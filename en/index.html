Frequently asked questions

What we are asked before signing

Is this an audit or a certification?

No. We prepare and document; attestation (CyFun, ISO, PASSI) stays with an accredited body. We never promise "guaranteed compliance". The French ReCyF framework is a working version and the French NIS2 transposition law had not been enacted as of 4 November 2026: we align your file with what exists today and update it with you.

What do I need to provide?

One hour of kick-off, the listed screenshots and exports (MFA, backup, EDR, inventory), and your validation of the documents. We provide templates and protocols.

Who works on my file?

Powix SAS is accountable; production is done by its team named in your contract, remotely, on EU-hosted virtual desktops.

What happens during the yearly follow-up?

A one-hour session each quarter, a dated report each time, your registers kept between sessions, and a 48-hour answer if you receive a questionnaire. You can stop at the end of any quarter.

What if I receive another questionnaire?

Included with the yearly follow-up (48 h). With file A alone, at the reduced "with file A" price shown on the pack B card.

Do you do penetration tests?

Not in 2026. Pack D is a public-surface analysis with a scan under written authorisation; we call it by its name.
